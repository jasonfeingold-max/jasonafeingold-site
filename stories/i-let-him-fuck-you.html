He would never see that waitress again. Even so, Frederica felt bad for her. She knew what it was like to perform a service without compensation.

They crossed the street back to the motel and got into Frederica’s car.

“Where to?” she asked as she stirred the engine to life.

“Just drive,” Edward said. “That way.” He pointed.

She did as she was told. They drove for about an hour, silent except for Edward barking out a direction every now and then. Frederica had no idea where she was. “When did I start being your whore?” Frederica asked.

“You were always my whore.”

The hills of Pennsylvania rolled by, grey and brown in the latter part of autumn, adding nothing good to the general mood inside the car.

She let herself into her car. She was free. The tricks she turned would be her own.
